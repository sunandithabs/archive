BICE · Behavioral Identity
Continuity Engine
Most intrusion detection looks at one moment at a time, so it misses anything that moves slowly enough to stay under every threshold. BICE keeps a running per-device behavioral baseline using Welford's online statistics and flags a device when its drift across DNS entropy, IP diversity, and port frequency crosses a threshold.

Evaluated on 89 N-BaIoT behavioral profiles across 9 physical devices: 100% TPR, 0% FPR. Getting there involved a few bugs that quietly faked good numbers along the way, full story on the project page.
