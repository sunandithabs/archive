ThreatIntelligence

This is the threat intel branch of the archive. Same approach as the systems writeups: primary sourcing where I can get it, explicit notes on where vendors disagree on numbers or naming, and a separate section for whatever I noticed that the reports didn't say outright. Ransomware tracking numbers move fast and different vendors count victims differently, so treat anything here as a snapshot, not a final count.

Ransomware · RaaS active since 2025

TheGentlemen

Possible Qilin splinter, first confirmed campaigns August 2025. Scaled from about 27 victims in month one to near 300 by mid-2026 per Halcyon. Heavy on GPO abuse and NETLOGON propagation for domain-wide hits, and the geography surprised me, only about 7% US share against an industry average closer to half.

Akira

Running under one name since March 2023, which is unusually long for this space. The aliases (Howling Scorpius, Punk Spider, Gold Sahara) are vendor naming for the same cluster, not separate affiliates, that one took some untangling. $244M+ in ransom proceeds per the November 2025 CISA advisory, mostly built off SMB targets rather than a few big scores.
